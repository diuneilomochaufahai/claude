## 4. Weaknesses you must manage

It is not a release, but Guo will say you settled. Answer: possession only; every email reserved rights; the sealing and dismissal terms show it was a move-out agreement.
- **Privity.** You were Li's subtenant, not Guo's tenant. Guo will argue no duty. Keep the case against Guo focused on post-notice conduct: 14 May onward.
- **The no-prosecution form.** You signed one on 2 September 2025. The one-year misdemeanor limit on Penal Code § 243.4(e)(1) expired on 2 September 2026. The criminal route for that incident is very likely closed. The civil route is wide open.
- **Collecting from Li.** He uses a P.O. box and three phone numbers and hides from service. A judgment against him may be slow to collect. Guo is the solvent, likely insured defendant.
- **Volume.** Your packets are strong but long. Every lawyer and the Appeals Unit need a two-page front. The appeal narrative in file 02 is that front.
- **Date conflicts to fix before anything is filed.** The restraining order 26PDRO00576 is dated 14 May 2026 in the Li packet, 1 May 2026 in the Guo packet, and your 22 June email reads as if it issued on 23 June. ## 5. Track A — The CRD appeal

**Deadline.** Ten calendar days from 2 October is Sunday 12 October. Do not rely on a weekend extension. **Send by Wednesday 7 October. Friday 9 October is the absolute latest.**

**How.** Download and complete the Appeal Request Form from calcivilrights.ca.gov/complaintprocess/?content=appeal. Attach the narrative in file 02. Email both to appeals@calcivilrights.ca.gov. Copy James Ramirez and Michael Ehrgott. Send a separate courtesy copy to Brenda Valle-Balderrama, who signed your certification and sits in the Appeals & Quality Assurance Unit. Request a read receipt and keep the sent email.

**What the Appeals Unit does.** It reviews whether the investigation was adequate and the determination supported by the file. It does not take new evidence. So the narrative argues from documents already in the file, by date, and asks for specific investigative steps.

**The seven grounds** (full text in file 02):

1. Guo's "no knowledge" defense was accepted although every act alleged against him post-dates 4 May.
2. The failure-to-accommodate claim was never analyzed; CRD declined to review medical records and never asked Guo about the request.
3. As to Li, "no independent witnesses" is the wrong standard, and the file's recordings contradict his "complied" claim; CRD never clarified what he denied despite your written question.
4. Allegations CRD confirmed were in the complaint (retaliation, housing terms, entries, shutoff, lockout, ads) were never put to Li.
5. The No Cause finding cannot be reconciled with CRD's own 21 July certification.
6. Specific investigative steps were not taken (police records, bodycam, Detective Lara's file, water district, City file, housemates, Guo and Cao interviews, a Cantonese-assisted interview of you after the rebuttal).
7. No reasons were given; request the case analysis.

**Rules for the appeal.** No new evidence. No attack on the analyst personally. No more than five or six pages plus an index. Specific, dated, calm. Ask for a written decision with reasons.

**After filing.** Expect 30 to 90 days. Follow up at three weeks and every three weeks after, by email. The appeal does not stop your court clock; treat the court deadlines in section 8 as fixed regardless of the appeal.
